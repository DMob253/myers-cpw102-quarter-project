# Product Requirements Document


## Problem

[Who has the problem? What is difficult for them today? Alternatively, what need do you see this fulfilling in which user group?]

Everyone needs car maintenance eventually, this would help to stay ahead of it and not be blindsided by costly repairs.

## Desired outcome

[What should be better or more interesting for users when this is done?]

This will make the users feel empowered knowing that repairing vehicles isn't all that difficult and keep them on top of maintenance intervals

## Scope

[Which users, use cases, and features will this app cover?]

I'm hoping to implement repir video links in the future for their specified vehicle, and take all the legwork out of researching themselves
 
## Out of Scope

[What features is this app specifically avoiding?]

I don't see my product needing any other features other than accepting user-input vehicle/make/model/year/mileage, and then whether or not they want to save their mileage at different repair intervals. It will be pretty basic.

## Requirements


### The product must

- Return the CORRECT maintenance information so as to not further damage a user's vehicle.

### The product should

- Have an up-to-date database to reference from

### The product could

- Implement repair video links on youtube or written repair information from sources like ALLDATA or ProDemand
